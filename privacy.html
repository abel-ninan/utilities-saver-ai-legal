Utilities Saver AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Effective October 11, 2026

The Utilities Saver AI app (the "app") is provided by the developer of Utilities Saver AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. Questions: UtilitiesSaver@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account. You never give us your name, email or password.
  • Your bill photos and PDFs stay on your iPhone. They're read on your device and never uploaded.
  • Setup stays on your iPhone. Answering the setup questions, adding a bill during setup and seeing your savings estimate use only your device. Nothing is sent for AI help during setup.
  • AI help is your choice, and we ask first. The first time you use an AI feature after setup (a Coach question, "Explain This Bill with AI", AI bill reading or rebate search), the app asks whether to use Google Gemini, a third-party AI service. Until you allow it, nothing is sent to Google or to our AI server.
  • Personal details are removed on your iPhone first. Before anything is sent for AI help, the app removes names, street addresses, account and meter numbers, phone numbers and email addresses that it detects.
  • Google's free API service. For now the app uses Google's free (unpaid) Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for that service, Google may use what it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. See section 2.1.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › AI help, and delete everything with Delete All Data.

1. Information that stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following only on your device: - bills you scan, photograph, import or type in, and the figures read from them - home profile answers - savings plan and action progress - guided-test results - smart-meter (Green Button) data you import - Coach conversations - settings, including your AI help choice

We have no copy of it.

  • Bill photos and PDFs are read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. By default the original image is deleted after its figures are read. If you turn on "Keep bill images", a reduced-size copy is kept on your device and is excluded from device backups.
  • Storage protection. App data uses iOS Data Protection ("complete protection"), so it can't be read while your iPhone is locked. Your saved figures (not bill images) may be included in your device's encrypted iCloud or computer backup, so your history survives a phone change.
  • Notifications (bill reminders, alerts) are scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

2. Information that leaves your iPhone, and why

2.1 AI help (only with your permission)

When we ask. The app never asks during setup. After you subscribe, the first time you start a feature that can use Google Gemini, the app shows a permission screen. It names Google Gemini, lists what would be sent, what is removed first and what is never sent, and offers two equal choices: - Allow AI Help: the features listed below may use Google Gemini. - Keep on iPhone: nothing is sent for AI help; the feature works on your device instead.

Closing the screen without choosing sends nothing. Your choice is saved with the date and the version of the explanation you saw. If what we send, who receives it, why, or how long it's kept changes materially, the app asks again before sending anything.

What is sent, if you allow it. Only what the feature you're using needs, from this list: - usage and dollar amounts from your bills - billing dates - utility and rate-plan names - line-item labels - your 5-digit ZIP code - your household answers (for example home size, heating type, number of occupants) - the questions you ask the Coach - for AI bill reading, the text read from your bill, with personal details removed

Personal details are removed on your device first. Your bill photos and PDFs are never sent. The features that can use Google Gemini are: reading and structuring your bills, explaining charges, writing your plan's explanations, answering Coach questions and searching for rebates and bill-help programs.

How it travels. Requests go over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, a function hosted by Supabase in the United States. The server passes them to Google and returns the answer. Our server does not store or log the content of your requests. Like most web services, our hosting provider automatically keeps basic technical records: - the time of a request - whether it succeeded - network information such as an IP address

These are kept for a short period, up to 7 days, for security and operations. These records don't include your bill figures or messages.

What Google does with it. Google receives requests from our server, not from your device, with no account or device identifier attached. For now, the app uses Google's free (unpaid) Gemini API service. Under Google's Gemini API terms for unpaid services, Google may use the content it receives, both what is sent and the answers, to provide, improve and develop its products and services, and human reviewers may read and annotate it. Because of this, please don't type sensitive personal information into the Coach. For rebate searches, which use Google Search to find current programs, Google stores the search request and result for 30 days to provide that feature. See Google's Gemini API terms at https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. If we move to Google's paid service, under which Google doesn't use this content to improve its products, we'll update this policy.

Without AI help. If you choose Keep on iPhone (or On this iPhone only in Settings), or haven't decided yet, no information is sent to Google or to our AI server: - Bills are read on your device, using Apple's on-device model when available, and you review every figure. - Coach answers on your device, with Apple's on-device model when available or with built-in answers calculated from your saved details. - Bill explanations use Apple's on-device model when available, or general explanations of each kind of charge. - Live rebate search is off; the app shows a list of trusted rebate and bill-help resources instead. - Your plan's numbers are always calculated on your device.

If Google Gemini can't answer when you've allowed it (for example because the free service's daily limit is reached or the service is busy), the app tells you and uses these on-device alternatives instead.

2.2 Public data lookups

To estimate weather effects, the app looks up weather for the approximate center point of your ZIP code area, never your address or bill details. Your iPhone requests weather history and forecasts directly from U.S. government services: the National Weather Service and NASA's POWER project. As with any website, these services can see your device's IP address. Rate, energy-price and solar figures come from public data built into the app (for example the OpenEI Utility Rate Database and the U.S. Energy Information Administration), so they don't need a request.

2.3 App protection

To keep our server from being abused, the app uses Apple's App Attest service. Your iPhone creates a key that proves requests come from the genuine app. To enforce fair-use limits, our server may keep a scrambled form of that key with request counts for up to 48 hours. It can't be used to identify you and is never combined with the content of your requests.

2.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information. The app checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

2.5 Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply, and we delete support emails within 12 months of resolving them.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't create accounts or ask for your name, email, phone number or street address.
  • We don't use advertising, third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We don't build profiles about you.
  • We don't use your information for any purpose other than providing the feature you asked for.
  • We don't use your information to train AI models. (Google's own use under its free-service terms is described in section 2.1.)
  • We don't access your location, contacts or photo library. You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, and only the photos you pick are read.

4. Third parties

The only services that receive information from the app are: - Google (Gemini API), for AI help, only with your permission, under the terms described in section 2.1. - Supabase, which hosts our server. - The public weather services listed above. - Apple, for purchases, App Attest and on-device features.

6. Your choices and rights

  • AI help: Settings › Privacy › AI help offers Ask me first (the default), Google Gemini and On this iPhone only. Choosing On this iPhone only takes effect immediately and stops requests in progress. Information already sent can't be recalled from Google, and Google's terms still apply to it.
  • Delete your data: Settings › Privacy › Delete All Data removes everything the app stores on your iPhone, including your AI help choice. Deleting the app also removes it. Deleting data doesn't cancel your subscription; manage that in your Apple Account settings.
  • Export: Settings › Privacy › Export My Data creates a copy you can save or share.
  • Access and deletion requests: we don't keep your bill data or link anything to you, so there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side. You can still contact us with any request and we'll respond within the time the law requires. Residents of states with consumer privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Texas) may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. We don't sell, share or target, and we'll honor any request that applies.

7. Children

The app is for adults 18 and older and isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has used AI help, contact us.

8. Security

  • Data on your device is protected by iOS encryption and Data Protection.
  • Information in transit uses HTTPS.
  • Our server has no database of user content.
  • The AI service key is kept only on our server, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, but we designed the app so that very little information exists outside your iPhone.

9. United States only

The app is offered only in the United States, and our server processes requests in the United States.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects what is sent for AI help, who receives it, why, or how long it's kept, the app will ask for your permission again before sending anything.
