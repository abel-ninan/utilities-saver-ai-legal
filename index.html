Support

Utilities Saver AI is a personal coach for your electricity, water and gas bills. It reads your bills on your iPhone, builds a plan of the changes most likely to lower your costs, and checks later bills to see what worked.

Contact us

Email UtilitiesSaver@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. There's no account. Bill photos and PDFs are read on your device and never uploaded. If you allow AI help, de-identified figures and redacted bill text are sent through our server to Google Gemini; our server stores nothing.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings › Privacy › Delete All Data. This removes everything the app stored on your iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Utilities Saver Pro. You can also open Settings › Subscription in the app and tap Manage Subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases.

Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are estimates based on your bills, your answers and typical homes. Actual results depend on your weather, rates, home and habits.
