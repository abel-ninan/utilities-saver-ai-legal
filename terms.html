Always confirm eligibility and terms with the program or utility. We don't run those programs and aren't responsible for their decisions.

  • If you smell gas, leave right away and call your gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • Set water heaters no higher than the manufacturer recommends; 120 °F is a common recommendation to reduce scald risk.
  • Follow your landlord's rules if you rent.
